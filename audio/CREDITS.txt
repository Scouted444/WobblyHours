Wobbly Hours audio

Sound effects
  Recorded foley from Kenney (www.kenney.nl), licensed CC0 (public domain):
    Impact Sounds, RPG Audio, Sci-Fi Sounds, Interface Sounds, UI Audio.
  Some files are layered / trimmed / levelled mixes of those recordings.
  Synthesised sounds (gunshot, splash, pop, burp, eating, drinking, horn, alarm, boing, UI chimes)
  were made for this game by tools/audio/make_sfx.py.

Music
  All 6 songs were composed and rendered for this game by tools/audio/make_music.py.
